Who We Are

Driving Generational Impact and Leadership Across Tanzania

Zanchangemakers is a premier youth-focused initiative dedicated to cultivating civic responsibility, practical workforce skillsets, and community-driven action across Zanzibar and the wider United Republic of Tanzania.

We work at the intersection of local community needs and emerging youth potential, offering pathways that transform passionate individuals into highly capable social change leaders, innovators, and modern workforce professionals.
